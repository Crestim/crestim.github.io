AID Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

AID ("the app") tells you whether an image or a video is likely to be AI-generated. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: your photos and videos never leave your device.

Who is responsible

Lorenzo Fumagalli
Contact: crestimsupport@gmail.com

Photos and videos you analyse

  • The analysis runs entirely on your device. The images and videos you choose or share to the app are not uploaded to any server and are not seen by us.
  • Files shared from another app are copied temporarily into the app's private storage and deleted after the analysis or after a short period (shared files waiting for a free analysis are kept for at most 7 days).
  • The app does not ask for access to your whole photo library: it uses the system picker or the content you share.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps on your device, in its private storage: your history of results (verdict, score, small preview thumbnail and date, up to 100 entries), your token balance, purchases already credited, and your settings (theme, language, tutorial seen). You can remove all of it by deleting the app. On iOS some of this data is kept in the Keychain and may survive a reinstall.

Notifications

The app uses local notifications to tell you when an analysis is finished. No notification data is sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob, to earn analysis tokens. To show ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process your advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses data and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before ads are loaded. You can change your choice from the app's settings, when the option is available.
  • iOS. The app asks for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you refuse, the app works the same and ads are not personalised.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Error reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

When the app hits a technical error, it may send a report to Google through Firebase Crashlytics. The report contains the type of error and where it occurred in the code, the device model, the operating system version, the state of the app and a random identifier of the installation. It does not contain your photos or videos, nor the content of the files you analyse. We use it only to find and fix stability problems, on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the app stable (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Reports are sent only by the version of the app published on the stores. Google processes this data under the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases and purchase verification

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

To make sure a purchase is genuine and is credited only once, the app sends our server the purchase proof given by the store (the Google Play purchase token or the App Store transaction identifier), the product and the store. The server checks it with Google Play or the App Store and answers with a signed confirmation. For this the app signs you in anonymously with Firebase Authentication the first time it contacts the server, when you make a purchase: no email, name or password is involved, only a random identifier.

  • What our server keeps for each verified purchase: the random identifier, the store, the product, the state (credited or refunded), the dates and, for the unlimited unlock, its expiry date. The purchase proof is kept only as a one-way hash.
  • Where. The server runs on Google Cloud (Cloud Functions and Firestore, region europe-west1, Belgium). Google processes this data on our behalf, under the Firebase privacy information.
  • Why. To provide the service you purchased (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, on the basis of our legitimate interest, to prevent fraud and the same purchase being used twice (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • How long. Purchase records are kept as long as they are needed to handle refunds and to prevent fraud.
  • Offline. If the server cannot be reached, the app keeps working and checks the purchase later. A purchase that is not verified within 7 days, or that the store refunds, loses its credit.

Your token balance and your history stay on your device.

What we do not do

  • No account, email or password is required. The only identifier on our server is the random anonymous one used to verify purchases.
  • No analytics in the app. The only diagnostic data is the technical error reports described above.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not collect your name, email address or location.

Residents of US states

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas or Virginia), you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to ask for its deletion and to opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted advertising.

  • We do not sell personal information. The only data we hold on our server is a random anonymous identifier linked to your purchases, as described above.
  • Google AdMob and its partners may use your advertising identifier and device information to show ads, which some laws consider "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can opt out from the privacy choices of the app (shown in the app's settings when your location requires it) or by limiting ad personalisation in your device settings.
  • We do not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

The only data we hold on our servers is the random anonymous identifier and the purchase records described above. To ask for access to or deletion of these records, write to crestimsupport@gmail.com with the receipt of your purchase from the store. Because the identifier is anonymous, we can act only on the records we are able to associate with the purchase you show us. For data processed by Google (AdMob and Firebase Crashlytics), you can exercise your rights (access, deletion, objection) with Google. If you are in the EEA, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. For any question, write to crestimsupport@gmail.com.
