AID Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

AID ("the app") tells you whether an image or a video is likely to be AI-generated. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: your photos and videos never leave your device.

Who is responsible

Lorenzo Fumagalli
Contact: crestimsupport@gmail.com

Photos and videos you analyse

  • The analysis runs entirely on your device. The images and videos you choose or share to the app are not uploaded to any server and are not seen by us.
  • Files shared from another app are copied temporarily into the app's private storage and deleted after the analysis or after a short period (shared files waiting for a free analysis are kept for at most 7 days).
  • The app does not ask for access to your whole photo library: it uses the system picker or the content you share.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps on your device, in its private storage: your history of results (verdict, score, small preview thumbnail and date, up to 100 entries), your token balance, purchases already credited, and your settings (theme, language, tutorial seen). You can remove all of it by deleting the app. On iOS some of this data is kept in the Keychain and may survive a reinstall.

Notifications

The app uses local notifications to tell you when an analysis is finished. No notification data is sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob, to earn analysis tokens. To show ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process your advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses data and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before ads are loaded. You can change your choice from the app's settings, when the option is available.
  • iOS. The app asks for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you refuse, the app works the same and ads are not personalised.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment details: the store only tells the app that a purchase was completed.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No analytics and no crash-reporting service in the app.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not collect your name, email address or location.

Residents of US states

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas or Virginia), you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to ask for its deletion and to opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted advertising.

  • We do not sell personal information and we do not hold personal data about you.
  • Google AdMob and its partners may use your advertising identifier and device information to show ads, which some laws consider "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can opt out from the privacy choices of the app (shown in the app's settings when your location requires it) or by limiting ad personalisation in your device settings.
  • We do not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you on our servers. For data processed by Google AdMob, you can exercise your rights (access, deletion, objection) with Google. If you are in the EEA, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. For any question, write to crestimsupport@gmail.com.
